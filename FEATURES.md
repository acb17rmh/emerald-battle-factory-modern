# Features

## Modern Pokémon sets

- Pokémon from Generations 1–9
- Modern battle mechanics and Pokémon data through Generation 9
- No Mega Evolution, Z-Moves, Dynamax, Gigantamax, or Terastallization
- Up to four distinct sets per species
- Viable movesets based on structured singles data
- Explicit abilities, including Hidden Abilities where appropriate
- Items, natures, EV spreads, and four legal moves for every generated set
- Support for modern moves and abilities provided by pokeemerald-expansion
- Filtering for unsupported gimmicks and incompatible competitive formats

## Quality of life

- Battle speed options
- Faster overworld movement
- Configurable text speed, including instant text
- Options for faster battle animations
